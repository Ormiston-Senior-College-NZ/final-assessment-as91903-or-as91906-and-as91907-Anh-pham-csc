WELCOME TO PUREBITE

WHERE YOU GAIN INTERESTING EXPERIENCE AND LEARN MORE ABOUT FOOD POISONING

PureBite is a web application that was created to provide information about food poisoning and introduce the users to a food compatibility checking tool, which can help the users to check whether they can combine two kinds of food together or not.


Guidance on how to use this web-app

- For a bigger and clearer picture, click on that picture to zoom it out. Click on the reverse arrow (the arrow pointing to the left on top of the laptop's browser) to close it.

- For further information, click on the link (underlined sentence) in the relevant report part.

- For checking food compatibility, use the compatible checker in the final page and follow the guidance.

- Any feedback, please send us an email by filling up the contact form in the footer.
