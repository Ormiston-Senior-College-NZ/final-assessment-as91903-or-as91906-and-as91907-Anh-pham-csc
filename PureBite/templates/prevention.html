1. Preserve food

- Fridges: As we store so much of our fresh food in the fridge, we must keep the fridge temperature between 2°C and 4°C; replace old or poor seals on doors; keep raw meat in the chiller or on the bottom shelf, away from other fresh foods; defrost frozen food in the fridge instead of the bench, but avoid getting thaw drips on other foods; keep food in the fridge covered in cling film or in containers with lids; and clean the fridge regularly.
- School lunches: Some tips to make sure their food stays healthy for them to eat are: if you are making lunches the night before, store them in the fridge; sandwiches with meat, fish, eggs, or mayo should be in an insulated lunch box; in the warmer months, freeze a drink bottle to pack in their lunchbox.
- Leftovers: To ensure leftovers are safe to eat, we need to make sure that leftovers should only stay in the fridge for 2-3 days before being eaten or thrown out; after defrosting, don’t refreeze leftovers a second time; reheat the food until it is steaming hot inside—and don’t reheat it a second time; and if the food in your fridge smells bad, it means it has gone off.

2. Process food

- Cooking tips: When preparing meals, we should use separate utensils for raw meat and fish than for cooked and ready-to-eat foods (this includes chopping boards, knives, and spatulas. If you don’t use different utensils, ensure you clean them thoroughly between tasks.); treat all raw chicken as if it carries food poisoning bacteria; keep it wrapped and separate in the fridge; washing knives, chopping boards, and anything they touch after use. (Even one drop of raw chicken juice can make you sick.)

3. Other tips

+ Practice Good Hygiene: Wash hands thoroughly before handling food and ensure that cooking surfaces and utensils are clean.
+ Cook Food Properly: Use a food thermometer to ensure that meats and eggs are cooked to safe internal temperatures.
+ Store Food Safely: Keep perishable items refrigerated and avoid leaving food out for extended periods.
+ Wash hands regularly: Practice good hygiene by washing hands before handling food and after using the restroom.
